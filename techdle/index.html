How to play

Guess the five-letter tech term in six tries. Every valid guess gives you clues:

  • 🟩 Green: right letter, right spot.
  • 🟨 Yellow: right letter, wrong spot.
  • ⬛ Grey: letter isn't needed (or you've used up its copies).

Every puzzle starts with a concept hint. Ask for up to two more hints if you get stuck. After the answer, you'll see a plain-English explanation and a practical example.
